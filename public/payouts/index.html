Faction Bank Payouts: Pay opens Torn's faction controls with the member, exact payout amount and Add to balance option pre-filled. Review the player and amount in Torn, then confirm the transaction yourself. The optional Auto-mark setting marks the member Paid as soon as Pay is clicked; it is off by default and remembered on this device. Tick Paid after the transfer is complete. Pay Next opens the next unpaid member.

Paid status is saved on this device for each War ID. Former members may need your faction's normal alternative payout process if Torn no longer allows their faction balance to be credited.

Whole-dollar payouts use a largest-remainder allocation so the member payouts add up exactly to the whole-dollar member pool.
